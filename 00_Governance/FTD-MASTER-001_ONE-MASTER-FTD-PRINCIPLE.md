## 2. CONSOLIDATION
When multiple FTDs exist:
1. Inventory all FTDs and FTD-like governance records.
2. Extract operative requirements, dependencies, decisions, exceptions and unresolved items.
3. Reconcile conflicts explicitly.
4. Consolidate operative rules into the Master.
5. Preserve original FTDs as **Historical Reference**; do not delete provenance.
6. Update the FTD Index so the Master is the active authority.
7. Link historical records with `SUPERSEDED-BY` / `HISTORICAL-REFERENCE`.
8. Preserve open, pending, blocked, verification and exception states.

Future governance requirements must be added to **FTD-MASTER-001**. Do not create duplicate FTDs for the same governance purpose. Temporary task FTDs must be reconciled into the Master after completion.
