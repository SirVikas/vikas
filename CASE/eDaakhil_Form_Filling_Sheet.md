**"Gist of Complaint / Brief Facts" box में paste करने के लिए तैयार टेक्स्ट (अंग्रेज़ी):**

> The Complainant holds ACKO Personal Health Policy No. ASHP202400001607 (04.11.2024–03.11.2025, Sum Insured ₹10,00,000; since renewed as ASHP202400001607R1). On 20.06.2025 he suffered acute urinary obstruction with infection and underwent emergency Circumcision + Meatoplasty at RS Maitri Health Care, Ghaziabad, on the day of admission itself, incurring ₹37,358. The insurer repudiated Claim No. 25062100350/1 on 26.06.2025 citing the 2-year Specific Illness waiting period, and had even declared on 25.06.2025 that the procedure "is not related to a malignancy" — five days BEFORE the histopathology report (30.06.2025) existed. The biopsy confirmed Balanitis Xerotica Obliterans (BXO), certified by the treating M.Ch. (AIIMS) Uro-Oncologist as a pre-malignant condition with risk of penile cancer; the policy's own waiting-period entry contains the exception "unless necessitated by malignancy". The Insurance Ombudsman (Award dated 02.07.2026) held medical necessity undisputed and the Permanent Exclusion inapplicable. The repudiation — made without examining the biopsy, without any medical opinion and contrary to the policy's own exception — is arbitrary, amounting to deficiency in service u/s 2(11) and unfair trade practice u/s 2(47) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

**"Relief Claimed" box के लिए तैयार टेक्स्ट:**
