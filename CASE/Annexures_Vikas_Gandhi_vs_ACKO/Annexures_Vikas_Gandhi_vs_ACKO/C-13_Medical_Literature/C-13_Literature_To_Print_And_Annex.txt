C-13 (colly.): MEDICAL LITERATURE - BXO/Lichen Sclerosus is a recognised
premalignant condition associated with penile squamous cell carcinoma.

PRINT the relevant pages of the following and place them in this folder:

1. EAU (European Association of Urology) Guidelines on Penile Cancer -
   section on epidemiology/risk factors (lichen sclerosus listed as a
   risk factor). https://uroweb.org/guidelines/penile-cancer

2. Nasca MR, Innocenzi D, Micali G. "Penile cancer among patients with
   genital lichen sclerosus." J Am Acad Dermatol 1999;41:911-914.
   (Malignant change in ~5.8% of men with genital lichen sclerosus --
   corroborates the "about 6%" stated at the Ombudsman hearing.)

3. Depasquale I, Park AJ, Bracka A. "The treatment of balanitis xerotica
   obliterans." BJU International 2000;86:459-465.
   (BXO's association with penile carcinoma; circumcision as treatment.)

4. WHO Classification of Tumours - Urinary and Male Genital Tumours
   (lichen sclerosus in the pathway of HPV-independent penile SCC).

TIP: Also request the treating specialist to countersign the printed
literature or reference it in a supplementary note.
