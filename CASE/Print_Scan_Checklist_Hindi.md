# Print / Scan / As-is PDF — Aasaan Checklist (Hindi)

**Case:** Vikas Gandhi vs ACKO General Insurance Ltd. — DCDRC Ghaziabad (e-Jagriti portal)
**Package:** `CASE/eJagriti_Filing_Package/`

Seedha funda ek line mein:

> **Jis kagaz par aapke SIGN (ya notary) chahiye — uska PRINT lo, sign karo, phir SCAN karke PDF banao.**
> **Jo pehle se PDF hai (email, policy, bill, report) — usmein kuchh nahi karna, AS-IS upload hoga.**

---

## 1️⃣ PRINT + SIGN + SCAN karne waale documents (6 files)

In sabka print lena hai, pen se sign karna hai, phir scan karke PDF banana hai:

| # | File | Kya karna hai |
|---|------|---------------|
| 1 | `01_Index.docx` | Print → paper-book ke page numbers bharo → sign → scan |
| 2 | `02_Proforma_for_Filing_Consumer_Complaint.docx` | Print → advocate row bharo (ya "Party-in-Person" likho) → sign → scan |
| 3 | `03_Synopsis_with_List_of_Dates_and_Events.docx` | Print → sign → scan |
| 4 | `04_Memo_of_Parties.docx` | Print → sign → scan |
| 5 | `05A_Consumer_Complaint_S35_CPA2019.docx` | Print → date bharo → **har page par sign** → Verification par sign → scan |
| 6 | `05B_Affidavit_in_Support_for_Notarisation.docx` | Print → **Notary ke saamne sign + notarise** → scan |

⚠️ **Complaint (5) + notarised Affidavit (6) ko milakar EK HI PDF banana hai** — portal par "Consumer Complaint with Notarised affidavit" ek hi item hai.

**Vakalatnama (`08_Vakalatnama.docx`)** — sirf tab print/sign/scan karna hai agar advocate rakh rahe hain (aap + advocate dono sign karenge). Party-in-Person hain to yeh step **skip**.

---

## 2️⃣ AS-IS PDF — na print, na scan (seedha upload)

`06_Annexures/Annexures_Vikas_Gandhi_vs_ACKO.zip` ke andar C-01 se C-14 tak ke saare documents **pehle se digital PDF hain** — policy schedule, premium receipts, discharge summary, hospital bills, claim/rejection/repudiation emails, biopsy report, appeals ka email thread, Ombudsman correspondence.

- In par koi sign nahi chahiye, koi print nahi chahiye.
- Bas **C-01 se C-14 order mein merge karke ek PDF** banao (size limit aaye to do hisse: C-01–C-08 aur C-09–C-14) aur upload karo.

---

## 3️⃣ Alag se SCAN karne waale (original kagaz jo abhi PDF nahi hain)

Annexure merge karne se **pehle** yeh 3 cheezein add karni hain:

| Annexure | Kya scan karna hai |
|----------|--------------------|
| C-12 | Doctor ke **original medical certificate** ka saaf scan |
| C-13 | Medical literature ke printouts (print lekar scan, ya seedha PDF save karo to print ki zaroorat hi nahi) |
| C-14 | **Aadhaar card** ki copy (scan/photo → PDF) |

---

## 4️⃣ Bilkul kuchh nahi karna (skip)

- `07_Application_IA_Note_and_Template.docx` — is filing ke liye IA **zaroori nahi**, portal par yeh step skip kar do.
- `00_READ_ME_FIRST_Filing_Guide.docx` — sirf padhne ke liye, upload nahi hota.
