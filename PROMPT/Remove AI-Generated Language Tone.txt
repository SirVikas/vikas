# Pull Request: Remove AI-Generated Language Tone from Legal Documents

## Summary

This PR refines the drafting style of legal documents to eliminate any wording or expression that could give the impression of AI-generated content.

## Problem

The previous drafts contained language patterns that, while grammatically correct, could appear overly polished, repetitive, or formulaic. In a courtroom setting, such language may create an unintended impression that the document was generated by AI rather than carefully drafted for the case.

## Changes Made

* Replaced artificial or generic expressions with natural legal drafting.
* Simplified sentence construction to resemble conventional legal pleadings.
* Removed repetitive and template-like phrases.
* Improved readability while preserving legal accuracy.
* Ensured the language reflects the style commonly used in court submissions.

## Objective

The document should read as a professionally drafted legal submission without conveying any indication that AI assistance was used in its preparation. The focus is on clarity, authenticity, and courtroom-appropriate language so that the Hon'ble Judge's attention remains on the merits of the case rather than the style of drafting.

## Expected Outcome

* Natural and human-like legal language.
* Courtroom-appropriate presentation.
* No AI-generated writing patterns or expressions.
* Better credibility and readability for judicial proceedings.
